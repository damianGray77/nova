# Behaviour profiles (A3)

The observer follows four bodies per node for 50 ticks every 1,000 ticks by
default. Its keyed sampling stream never advances a simulation stream.
Profiles record executed codons, op counts, actions and their outcomes,
disk kind and tier, holdings, lineage, genotype, origin, neighbours, and
LISTEN values followed by branches. Arrivals have a separate CSV for the
moving-together measure. Output paths are settings in the registry.

The live census uses fixed action rules. Offline `nova analyse roles`
scales action features, selects k by silhouette, names clusters by their
leading features, and writes census and working-code complexity reports.
`nova analyse groups` compares moving together, kin clustering and
listening then branching against shuffled or analytic chance baselines.
These are observational associations, not evidence of causal cooperation.

Tests cover planted predators, parasites and copiers, exact executed-code
bitmaps, CSV round trips, planted versus shuffled group effects, and equal
hashes with profiles on/off at 1, 4 and 20 threads and shuffled dispatch.
Snapshot resume preserves the world hash; observer samples start afresh.
Release sampling costs are recorded in the final integration results.
